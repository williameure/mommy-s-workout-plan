Every session

Warm-up, 4 min: 10 chin tucks · 8 cat-cows · 15 band pull-aparts · 15 glute bridges · 8 open-books per side.

Exhale on the hard part of every rep. Stop the set if the midline domes, or if there's leaking or heaviness.

Progress in order: reps to the top of the range → add a set → add weight and reset the reps.

Daily, off days included: chin tucks ×10 three times, doorway pec stretch 30 sec per side, screens at eye level.
